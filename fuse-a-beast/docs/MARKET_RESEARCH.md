# Market Research & Gap Analysis — Roblox, 2026

> Phases 1 & 2 of the founder brief. This is the "why", not just the "what".
> Research conducted July 2026 from public sources (linked at the bottom).

## 1. The state of the platform (2026)

- **The money is real and growing.** Roblox bookings hit **$2.2B in Q4 2025** (+63% YoY), and **creator payouts reached $1.5B in 2025** (up from $923M in 2024). The top 10 creators averaged ~$38.5M each.
- **A single breakout can move the whole platform.** *Grow a Garden* pushed Roblox past **100M+ users** and hit a reported **~21.9M concurrent players** — the biggest CCU in gaming history. *Steal a Brainrot* is the only game to cross **25M CCU**.
- **Cosmetics-first monetization won.** *Rivals* (an FPS) monetizes almost entirely on skins + battle passes. Platform-wide, players in 2026 spend more on **cosmetics, status items, and trading-relevant rarities** than on time-skips.
- **Mobile is the floor, not the ceiling.** Every top game runs well on a budget phone.

## 2. What the breakout hits actually teach us

We studied the winners to extract *transferable principles*, not clonable content.

### Grow a Garden — the retention machine
- **Offline/idle progression.** It *rewards stepping away* — your garden grows while you're gone. This is unusual on Roblox (most sims punish you for logging off) and it's the single biggest driver of its return habit.
- **Layered unlocks.** New systems reveal over time so the game keeps "feeling fresh."
- **5-minute shop refreshes + weekly limited events** create urgency and a hunt loop.
- **"6-to-99" simplicity.** Anyone understands it in seconds.

### Steal a Brainrot — the social/competitive tension machine
- **A tight loop: acquire → grow → defend → steal.** Value constantly shifts; fortunes swing fast.
- **Meme-fueled collectibles** are inherently shareable → organic TikTok/YouTube growth.
- **Social by default** — servers are full of players reacting to each other.

### Blox Fruits / Rivals — the depth + cosmetics machine
- **Long session times + loyal communities** (RPG/combat depth).
- **Cosmetic ladders** (skins, battle passes) that never break the competitive economy.

### The common DNA
1. **A dead-simple core loop** you grasp in seconds.
2. **A reason to come back tomorrow** (idle growth, streaks, limited events).
3. **A shareable moment** (a rare drop, a big steal) that markets the game for free.
4. **Cosmetic/status/rarity monetization** that respects — and often *feeds* — a trading economy.
5. **Relentless weekly updates** driven by community feedback.

## 3. Highest-signal findings by dimension

| Dimension | Finding |
|---|---|
| Highest-earning genres | Simulators (pet/collection/progression), RPG, roleplay/avatar, tycoon |
| Fastest-growing | Idle/collection sims with viral loops; meme-collectibles; horror hybrids |
| Highest retention | Games with **offline progression**, streaks, and daily limited content |
| Session length | RPG/roleplay longest; idle sims win on **frequency** of return, not length |
| Monetization | Gamepasses = 60-70% of surface (permanent, aspirational); dev products = 30-40% (repeatable). Convert hardest at **25-99 R$** and **400-1000 R$** tiers |
| Trending mechanics | Offline growth, collection/rarity chase, "steal/defend", fusion/merge |
| UI patterns | **Invisible onboarding** (no tutorial gate), one-tap core action, mobile-first |
| Update cadence | **Weekly** content + limited-time events with countdowns |
| Why games fail | Tutorial overload; no Day-2 hook; poor mobile UX; exploitable economies; monetizing before Day-7 retention >15% |
| Low-competition/high-demand | Fashion/social (huge, underserved), horror-tycoon hybrids, **discovery/combination crafting** (rare on top charts), co-op-with-progression |

**Retention is the whole game.** Over **80% of lifetime revenue comes from players who survive the first week**, and most games lose **>50% of players before Day 2**. So: fix retention first, monetize second.

## 4. Gap analysis — where the opportunity is

We are explicitly **not cloning**. We look for underserved niches, repeatedly-requested mechanics, and *unexplored genre combinations*:

- **Discovery / combination crafting** ("mix things to reveal new things") is proven addictive off-platform (Little Alchemy, Infinite Craft) but **rare among Roblox's top earners**. It has built-in "gotta-discover-'em-all" retention and generates shareable "I found X!" moments for free.
- **Offline idle progression** is still uncommon outside *Grow a Garden* — a durable retention edge.
- **Collection + rarity + trading** is the monetization engine of 2026 and pairs naturally with discovery.
- **Merge/level mechanics** give duplicates value (a common complaint in collection games is "dupes are useless").

The unexplored **combination** is the opportunity: **idle generation → discovery-fusion → collection/rarity → display/social → merge/trade**, wrapped in the on-trend imperative-verb naming convention.

## 6. The choice: **Fuse a Beast** (idea #1)

**Why it wins on expected ROI:**

- **Demand (5):** collection + idle + rarity are the three highest-retention/-revenue patterns on the platform, and it's mobile-first tap gameplay.
- **Viral (5):** every new discovery is a shareable moment; signature "recipes" become community/TikTok knowledge ("fuse Fire+Air+Void for the Chronodragon!").
- **Revenue (5):** cosmetics/luck/convenience monetization + a trading-ready rarity economy — exactly what 2026 spenders pay for — without pay-to-win.
- **Competition (4/underserved):** discovery-fusion is proven addictive but rare among Roblox top earners; we're first-mover in an on-trend combo rather than 400th into pet-hatching.
- **Dev time (4) / Difficulty (4):** the core loop is a data-driven weighted roll over element inputs — **cheap to build, cheap to expand** (new beasts/recipes/events are pure config). This is what makes weekly updates sustainable, which the research says is decisive.

**How we avoid cloning:** we take *principles* (offline growth, discovery, rarity, weekly events) and combine them in a configuration no top game currently occupies — element-shard idle generation feeding a discovery-fusion codex with merge-for-power and a social display sanctuary.

**Launch strategy (per the iteration guidance):** ship a *polished core loop* — generate shards → fuse → discover → display for idle boost → chase rarer beasts — then add weekly beasts/recipes, limited events, and trading as fast-follows driven by player data.
